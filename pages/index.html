BROWSER-BASED SQUAD TACTICS ROGUELIKE

Four teammates.
One action.
Make it count.

Demonthrone puts your squad on an unbounded, isometric voxel battlefield. Find a sightline. Choose your ground. Spend the team’s one action where it matters most.

01 / THE GAME

The ground is part
of the fight.

01

One choice for the whole squad

Your vanguard, warden, varmint, and marauder have different strengths. Moving, pushing, attacking, or enchanting spends the team’s shared action. Choose who acts; live with what follows.

02

What you see matters

Fog of war conceals the battlefield. Elevation, boulders, walls, and living characters block sightlines. A target being close is no guarantee you can hit it.

03

A world beyond the screen

Explore six procedural biomes: cinder, fen, heath, ridge, bog, and mesa. Rivers slow your advance, ice changes movement costs, and climbable brick walls follow the landscape.

04

More than move and attack

Push crates into position or enchant them into follower chains. Inspect enemy reach before committing. Pursuers and towering nephilim bring different threats to your next turn.

02 / YOUR FIRST TURN

Read the field.
Commit your move.

No installation or account required. Open the prototype in a browser with WebGL support. A desktop mouse and keyboard are the easiest way to start.

Enter the battlefield ↗

  1. Select a teammate

    Click a squad member to see where they can move and which targets they can attack.

  2. Plan one action

    Click a highlighted destination or target. Preview the choice before you commit. Press Esc to cancel and rethink.

  3. Resolve the turn

    Press Enter or click go. Your plan resolves, then enemies attack and pursue. Read the new position and choose again.

Look around

Drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag to rotate and tilt, or use the on-screen rotation buttons. Click an enemy to inspect its movement and attack range.

03 / BUILT IN THE OPEN

Early days.
Real decisions.

This is a playable proof of concept, not a finished game. The terrain, visibility, combat, and crate systems are here to try now. Save and load, inventory, and richer character inspection are planned; they are not in the prototype yet.

Try a few turns. Tell us what surprised you, where you got stuck, and what you want to see next.
